# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- WASAPI loopback system-audio visualizer.
- Custom 2048-point FFT, Hann window, dB mapping, log-frequency bars, peak hold.
- Themes, gradients, tints, ASCII mode, mirror mode, and color-depth options.
- Test-tone diagnostic source.
- Per-application capture and dual-app split view.
